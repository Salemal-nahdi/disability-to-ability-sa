About Us

About Disability to Ability SA.

With over 25 years working in the healthcare industry, Sarah felt there was a better way to deliver services and support to people living with a disability.

So after working for big organisations including Royal Adelaide Hospital, Sarah decided to start her own business in the disability sector to offer help and support for people with a disability or psychosocial disability.

To fund the business, Sarah and her husband sold their property and Disability to Ability SA was born.

With a loving and caring team supporting people across South Australia, Disability to Ability SA is a well known and trusted disability service in Adelaide.

Offering a range of services from in-home care to animal experiences, our goal is to empower and give those with a disability, the ability to live a fulfilling life.

Meet The Team

Sarah

CEO

Sarah is the founder of Disability to Ability SA. With over 25 years’ experience in the healthcare industry, she has worked across aged care, nursing, community, and disability sectors.

Sarah’s love for animals has inspired the development of the Animal Program, with her ever-growing four-legged family playing a special role. She remains actively involved in the day-to-day delivery of services, continuing to support participants through face-to-face involvement across all programs. Sarah’s passion and drive to make a meaningful difference in people’s lives continues every day.
